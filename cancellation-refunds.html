Cancellation & Refunds Policy

At La Spa, customer satisfaction is our priority. You may request a cancellation within 24 hours of placing your order. Refund requests must be made within 7 days of order receipt.

Refunds are processed within 7-10 business days after approval. The amount will be credited to the original payment method.

Products must be returned in original condition. Shipping charges may apply as per company policy.
